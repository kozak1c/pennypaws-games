Giggle Bomb Privacy Policy

Penny Paws Games · Effective 14 November 2026

The short version

  • Giggle Bomb is a party game for one phone that a family passes around. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no online play and no way for players to share anything.
  • We (Penny Paws Games) run no servers and keep no information about you or your child. The only information that leaves the device is technical information the Google ad software sends to Google, and what Google Play needs for a purchase, both described below.
  • Players' names, voice recordings, the dares you write and game progress are saved only on your device. We never receive them.
  • The game uses the microphone only when a player taps to record their name or a yell.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, never during a round. Every ad request is marked as coming from a child, so the ads are not personalized and are limited to content rated for general audiences.
  • The optional Giggle Pass is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see payment details.
  • Buying the Pass and the game's link to this policy sit behind a grown-up check.

This policy covers the Giggle Bomb app for Android, installed from Google Play.

What stays on your device

The game saves these things on your device so your family can pick up where you left off:

  • The players you make. For each one, the game keeps the name typed for them, their animal and colour, whether they get kid or grown-up questions, what Sparky (the game's announcer) calls them, and the silly voice and loudness picked for their recordings. Sparky calls a player by a nickname picked from the game's list or by their own recording; he does not read out typed names. We suggest a first name or a nickname rather than a full name.
  • Voice recordings, if a player makes any (see the next section).
  • The dares your family writes yourselves, a Giggle Pass feature.
  • Gems, unlocked games and dare packs, things bought in the gem shop and who unlocked them, best scores, the last champion, who is sitting at the table, and your settings.
  • Whether the Giggle Pass is owned, and how many double-gems videos were watched today, which the game uses for its daily limit.

This information stays on the device and is never sent to us. The game switches off Android's backup for its data, so it is not copied to your Google account.

You can delete what the game saved:

  • To remove a player, tap PLAY!, then tap ✏️ Edit and the player's name, then tap the bin button 🗑️. This deletes the player and their recordings.
  • To delete one recording, open the player's card, tap 🎤 My voice, pick My name or Splat yell, and tap the bin button 🗑️.
  • To delete a dare you wrote, open Settings (⚙️), tap 🎭 Dares and tap ✖ next to the dare.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved.

The game has no single button that deletes everything at once.

The microphone and voice recordings

A player can record their own name, so Sparky calls them by it, and a short “splat yell” that plays when they go boom. Recording is optional. Everything else in the game works without it, and a player can pick a nickname from the game's list instead.

Recording happens only in the game's voice booth (on a player's card, tap 🎤 My voice), and only after the player taps the red button. The microphone turns off again after 2.5 seconds for a name or 3 seconds for a yell, or sooner if the player taps to stop. The first time, Android asks whether the game may use the microphone. If you say no, recording stays off and the rest of the game works as usual. You can change this later in your phone's settings. The microphone is the only permission the game asks you to allow.

Each recording is saved as a sound file in the game's private storage on the device. The game keeps the original and a copy changed to the silly voice the player picks. Recordings are only played back on the device, in the game. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

To record again, tap 🎤 Say it again in the voice booth. To delete a recording, tap the bin button 🗑️ there. Removing a player deletes their recordings too.

What we collect

Nothing ourselves. Giggle Bomb does not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, photos, contacts or location, and it does not use the camera. Players type names and may record their voices, as described above, but these stay on the device and we never receive them. The game has no analytics or tracking tools of our own, and we run no servers that receive information from the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Giggle Bomb is free and shows ads from Google AdMob to players who do not have the Giggle Pass. Ads never play during a round. There are two places they can appear:

  • After a match, a player can choose to watch a video to double the gems from that match. It is offered once per match and at most 4 times a day.
  • A short ad break can appear while a player acts out a dare away from the phone. It starts a few seconds after Sparky reads the dare out, and only on dares that do not use the phone. There is no ad break before the second boom of a session, none within 3 minutes of another ad, and none in solo games.

If no ad is ready, the game simply carries on. Ads play with their sound off. The Giggle Pass removes all ads.

Every ad request from the game is marked as directed to children under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent in Europe, and is limited to ads rated for general audiences. With these settings we instruct Google not to show personalized (interest-based) ads or remarketing and to limit the information it uses, as Google describes in its child-directed ads documentation. The game does not request the Android advertising ID permission, or Android's Privacy Sandbox permissions for ad topics and ad measurement.

To show ads, count them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the game sends Google technical information such as the device's IP address, device model, operating system and language, the app's version, which ads were shown or tapped, and app-level identifiers Google uses to count ads and prevent fraud (not the advertising ID). Google's list is at developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure. Google uses this information as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We do not receive this information. The game never gives the ad software players' names, recordings or dares.

If you see an ad that seems wrong for children, email us the date, the approximate time and what it showed, and we will report it to Google and block the advertiser.

Purchases (Google Play)

The Giggle Pass is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not see or store names, email addresses or payment details. The game only receives Google Play's confirmation that the Pass was bought, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device.

Buying the Pass always goes through a grown-up check first (a multiplication question), and Google Play may ask for your Google account password or fingerprint as well. Restoring a Pass you already bought does not need the check, because it buys nothing.

Outside links

Apart from the ads themselves, the game has one link that leaves it: a link to this policy, behind the same grown-up check.

Children's privacy

Giggle Bomb is made for children and the grown-ups who play with them. A child can type a name and record their voice in the game, for the game's own use on the device. That information never leaves the device, and we never receive it. The game gives children no way to share personal information. The only information collected from children is the technical information Google needs to show ads, count them and prevent fraud. We rely on COPPA's support-for-internal-operations exception for this: it is never used to contact a child, build a profile or show behaviourally targeted ads.

Because we hold no information about your child, there is nothing for us to review or delete. We keep no personal information, so we have no retention period. Google keeps the technical ad information for the periods described in its privacy policy. Names, recordings, dares and progress saved on the device can be deleted by you at any time as described above. If you have questions or concerns, email us and we will help.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may collect information such as install counts and crash reports under Google's own policies and your device settings. Google shows us aggregated reports (install counts, crash reports by device model and Android version, ad counts and earnings, and order numbers with country and amount for refunds). We do not use them to identify anyone.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as California law defines those terms.

We do not hold any personal information about you, and the data on your device is under your control. For information Google holds, use the tools in your Google account. Where EU or UK law applies, Penny Paws Games is the controller for the ad information collected in the game, and Google LLC (USA) processes it under its own terms. Google transfers data to the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. You may complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).
