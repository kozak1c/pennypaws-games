Squishy Bop! Privacy Policy

Penny Paws Games · Effective 20 November 2026

The short version

  • Squishy Bop! has no accounts, no sign-in and no chat. Its Share button sits behind the grown-up check and opens your device's own share menu with a short message we wrote and a link to the game's Google Play page; the message cannot be changed in the game.
  • We (Penny Paws Games) run no servers and keep no information about you or your child. The only information the game sends from the device is technical information the Google ad software sends to Google and the Squishy Pass purchase checks that go through Google Play, both described below.
  • Game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Every ad request is marked as coming from a child, so the ads are not personalized and are limited to content rated for general audiences. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland Google shows only its "limited ads", and the game shows no consent form to anyone.
  • The optional Squishy Pass is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see payment details.
  • Purchases, the grown-up settings, the Share button and the link to this policy sit behind a grown-up check. The Rate button and the one-time rating card do not; they open Google Play, described below.

For kids

  • Your game is saved only on your phone or tablet. We never see it.
  • The game never asks for your name, where you live or a photo of you.
  • The game shows ads from Google. To send an ad, Google gets a few facts about the phone or tablet, like its kind, its language and its internet address, and which ads were shown. The ads are not chosen by what you like or what you did before.
  • Buying something or sharing the game needs a grown-up.
  • If anything in the game worries you, show a grown-up you trust.

Some versions of the game, including early test versions on Google Play, contain no advertising or purchase software. In those versions the Ads and Purchases sections below do not apply and the game sends no information from your device. Versions from outside Google Play also have no Rate or Share buttons, so that section does not apply to them.

Who we are

If you have a question about this policy or your child's privacy, you can reach us by email at pennypawsgames@gmail.com, by phone at +1 586-929-9644, or by mail at 4806 Rockport Dr, Durham, NC 27703, USA. Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland can find more in the section for them below.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress on your device so you can pick up where you left off: scores, stickers, collected squishies, levels, settings, whether the Squishy Pass is owned, whether the rating card has been shown or Rate tapped, and counters and a short log of when ads were offered and shown, which the game uses to space out ads. This information stays on the device and is never sent to us. The game switches off Android's backup for its data, so it is not copied to your Google account.

You can delete all progress at any time: open the pause menu, tap Grown-ups, then Wipe progress. Wipe progress keeps whether the Pass is owned, the graphics setting and whether the rating card has been shown. Uninstalling the game deletes everything it saved.

What we collect

Nothing ourselves. Squishy Bop! does not ask for or collect names, email addresses, photos, voice, contacts or location. It does not use the camera or microphone. It has no analytics or tracking tools of our own, and we run no servers that receive information from the game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Squishy Bop! is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Some ads are optional videos a player can choose to watch for a reward, such as an extra rescue. Short ad breaks appear only between games, never during play. The Squishy Pass removes ad breaks.

Every ad request from the game is marked as directed to children under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent in Europe, and is limited to ads rated for general audiences. With these settings we instruct Google not to show personalized (interest-based) ads or remarketing and to limit the information it uses, as Google describes in its child-directed ads documentation. The game does not request the Android advertising ID permission.

To show ads, count them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads software in the game sends Google technical information such as the device's IP address (from which Google can estimate the general area, not a precise location), device model, operating system and language, the app's version, when the app was opened and which ads were shown or tapped, performance information such as start-up time, freezes and energy use, and app-level identifiers Google uses to count ads and prevent fraud (not the advertising ID). Google's list is at developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure. Google uses this information as described in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services. We do not receive this information.

If you see an ad that seems wrong for children, email us the date, the approximate time and what it showed, and we will report it to Google and block the advertiser.

Tapping an ad can open Google Play or a web page outside the game. Ads are not behind the grown-up check.

Purchases (Google Play)

The Squishy Pass is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not see or store names, email addresses or payment details. The game only receives Google Play's confirmation that the Pass was bought, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new device.

Buying or restoring the Pass always goes through a grown-up check first, and Google Play may ask for your Google account password or fingerprint as well.

Rate and Share

The Google Play version of the game has a Rate button and a Share button in Settings. Share asks the grown-up check first. Rate does not, so a child can tap it. After a few days of play, the game may also show a card, one time, inviting a rating. Once Google Play has told the game that it prices the Squishy Pass in the currency of a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game does not show that card; until Google Play has answered (for example when the device is offline) the card can still appear. The card is not behind the grown-up check either. It has a Rate button, which opens the same Google Play page, and a Not now button. Once the card has been shown, or Rate has been tapped, the game does not show it again on that device unless the game is reinstalled or its data is cleared in Android's settings.

Rate opens the game's page in the Google Play Store app, or on the Google Play website. Anything done there, such as leaving a rating or a review, happens in Google Play under Google's own terms and privacy policy, with the Google account on the device. The game does not see whether a rating or review was left. Reviews are public on Google Play, and Google shows them to us with the name on the Google account that wrote them. Please do not put a child's name or other personal details in a review.

Share, once the grown-up check is answered, opens your device's own share menu with a fixed message: the game's name, one sentence about it and a link to its Google Play page. The message cannot be changed in the game, and it holds no information about the player. The game does not send the message anywhere itself and sends nothing to us. If someone picks an app from the menu, the message is handed to that app. What happens next, including who it goes to and what they can see about the sender, is up to that app (for example a messaging or email app) and its own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Squishy Bop! is made for children and the grown-ups who play with them. The game does not ask a child for a name, contact details or any other personal information, and we collect none ourselves. The Share button sits behind the grown-up check and only hands the fixed message described above to the app someone picks; anything added or sent in that app, and who it goes to, happens in that app and not in the game. The grown-up check is a sum written in words, answered on a number pad. It is there to keep young children out of these parts of the game. It cannot prove that a grown-up answered, and an older child who reads and adds well could pass it. The only information collected from children is the technical information Google needs to show ads, count them and prevent fraud. We rely on COPPA's support-for-internal-operations exception for this. We make sure of it by marking every ad request as child-directed and by removing the advertising ID permission, so that under Google's child-directed rules it is not used to contact a child, build a profile or show behaviourally targeted ads.

Because we hold no information about your child, there is nothing for us to review or delete. We keep no personal information from the game, so we have no retention period for it. Google keeps the technical ad information for the periods described in its privacy policy. Progress saved on the device can be wiped by you at any time as described above. To stop the ad software sending anything more, uninstall the game or play with the device offline. The Squishy Pass removes ad breaks but does not switch the ad software off. If you have questions or concerns, email us and we will help.

Google Play and Android

Google Play and Android may collect information such as install counts and crash reports under Google's own policies and your device settings. Google shows us aggregated reports (install counts, crash reports by device model and Android version, ad counts and earnings, and order numbers with country and amount for refunds), and the public ratings and reviews left on Google Play. We do not use them to identify anyone.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as California law defines those terms.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information. The section below sets out the rights of players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland. Apart from any email you send us, we do not hold any personal information about you, and the data on your device is under your control. For information Google holds, use the tools in your Google account. You can still contact us with any request and we will respond. If you email us, we use your address only to reply.

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland

This section applies where the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection applies to the game.

Who is responsible. Corey Kozak, trading as Penny Paws Games (contact details above), is the controller for the information the game's ad software sends to Google. Google decides for itself how it uses that information, as a separate controller, under its own terms and privacy policy, which names the Google company responsible for your information.

Representatives. We have no establishment in the European Union or the United Kingdom, and we have not yet appointed the representatives that Article 27 of the GDPR and of the UK GDPR call for. Until we have, Squishy Bop! is not offered on Google Play in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom; when we have, their names and addresses will be added here. We have not appointed a representative in Switzerland.

Ads in these countries. Every ad request from the game is marked as coming from a child under the age of consent and carries no consent signal, so Google shows only what it calls limited ads. Google does not use personal data to personalize these ads, the advertising ID is not sent, there is no remarketing, and Google switches off requests to third-party ad vendors such as ad measurement pixels and third-party ad servers. The ads themselves can still come from other advertisers through Google. Google's ad software still uses the device's IP address to deliver the ad (and can estimate the general area from it, not a precise location) and may keep the ad's files on the device to show it. Depending on our ad settings, Google may also store information on the device only to detect invalid traffic and fraud. The game never asks Google for personalized ads, so it shows no consent form to anyone.

Our legal bases. For the ad information: our legitimate interests in funding a free game with non-personalized ads and in keeping those ads free of fraud (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR and of the UK GDPR). We weighed these against children's interests and limited the processing for that reason: no personalization, no advertising ID, nothing sent to us, and ad breaks only between games. For the Squishy Pass: Google Play sells it under its own terms; the game only reads Google Play's confirmation on the device so it can unlock what was bought (Article 6(1)(b), the purchase). For emails you send us: to answer you (Article 6(1)(f), or Article 6(1)(b) when it is about a purchase). For the order records and reviews Google Play shows us: handling refunds and our accounts (Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).

Your rights. You can ask for access to your information, for it to be corrected or deleted, for its use to be restricted, and for a copy you can take elsewhere. We hold no information about players apart from emails sent to us and what Google Play shows us (orders and public reviews, described above), so for the ad information Google is the one holding it: use the tools in your Google account, or write to us and we will help. We answer requests within one month (30 days in Switzerland); if a request is complex the law lets us take longer, and we will tell you if so.

Your right to object. You can object at any time to the use of information based on our legitimate interests (the ads). We cannot pick out one player in Google's ad information, so the way to stop it is to uninstall the game or play with the device offline; write to us and we will help with anything else.

Complaints. You can complain to us first at the email above; we will acknowledge it within 30 days. You can also complain to a data protection authority: in the UK the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), in the European Economic Area the authority of your country (listed at edpb.europa.eu), and in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch).

Transfers outside Europe. The ad and purchase information goes to Google, including in the United States and the other countries where Google runs data centres, listed in its privacy policy. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, its UK Extension and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework, and Google also relies on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for transfers.

Keeping and deciding. We keep no game information; Google keeps the ad and purchase information for the periods in its privacy policy. Emails you send us are kept in our Gmail mailbox, in the United States, for as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of the request. The game makes no automated decisions about anyone and builds no profile.

Children's Code. We have checked the game against the UK Information Commissioner's Age Appropriate Design Code. Everyone gets the most private settings, with no setting to lower them, no location feature and no profiling.

Changes to this policy

When we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and its effective date.
