Website Privacy Policy

Penny Paws Games · Effective 18 November 2026

The short version

  • This website has no accounts, sign-in, forms, comments, ads, cookies, analytics scripts or tracking tools.
  • It loads nothing from other websites and stores nothing in your browser.
  • Cloudflare, which hosts the site, handles the technical details every website receives, such as your IP address, to deliver pages and keep the site safe. What Cloudflare shows us is almost all totals, such as how many times a page was visited.
  • We sell nothing on this site. When you follow a link to Etsy, Amazon or Google Play, that store's own privacy policy applies. Our Etsy links tell Etsy the visit came from us, as described below.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to reply and keep a record of our conversation.

Who we are

This website, pennypawsgames.com, is run by Penny Paws Games, the trade name of Corey Kozak, an individual in the United States. You can reach us by email at pennypawsgames@gmail.com, by phone at +1 586-929-9644, or by mail at 4806 Rockport Dr, Durham, NC 27703, USA.

This policy covers the website only. Each of our games has its own privacy policy: Squishy Bop!, Plush Hour and Giggle Bomb. The page that personalizes printables bought on Etsy, print.pennypawsgames.com, has its own privacy policy.

What the site collects

Nothing that you type or that names you. The site never asks for your name, email address or any other personal information. (The technical information every website receives is described under Hosting.) It sets no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and contains no analytics, advertising, social media or other tracking code. Every picture, video, font and script on the site is served from the site itself.

Do Not Track. The site does not track you over time or across other websites, and lets no one else do so through it. So it treats every visitor the same, whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Hosting (Cloudflare)

The site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. Like any website, each time your browser asks for a page, the request carries technical information such as your IP address, the address of the page, your browser and device type, and the time. Cloudflare uses this to deliver the page and to protect the site against attacks and abuse, and keeps it in its own logs for a limited time under its privacy policy. The reports Cloudflare gives us are totals, such as the number of visits to each page and broad country counts. Its security and troubleshooting tools can also show us the technical details of single requests, for example the IP address of one it blocked. We do not use any of this to identify visitors.

Links to stores and other sites

The site links to the stores that sell our products (Etsy, Amazon and Google Play) and to our pages on Instagram and Facebook. When you follow one of these links, you leave this site, and that company's privacy policy applies to what happens there. Your browser tells the site you arrive at that you came from pennypawsgames.com, but not which of our pages you were on.

Our links to Etsy are Etsy “Share & Save” links, so Etsy can tell that your visit came from our link. If you then buy from our Etsy shop within 30 days, Etsy credits part of its fees back to us. You pay the same price. Etsy shows us the number of clicks and orders from our links; it does not tell us who clicked. If you buy, the order we see (with the details Etsy gives every seller, under Etsy's privacy policy) is marked as having come from our link. Etsy may use cookies on its own site to remember the visit.

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and your message. We use them only to reply to you and to keep a record of the conversation. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail), which stores it under Google's privacy policy. We keep messages for as long as we need them to help you. Ask us from the same email address and we will delete yours, unless we have to keep a message for a legal reason such as an open dispute.

Children

The site shows games, printables and books made for children, and it is meant to be browsed by grown-ups and children together. It never asks a child for anything. The only information collected from any visitor, children included, is the technical information described under Hosting, which Cloudflare needs to deliver pages and keep the site safe. We rely on COPPA's support-for-internal-operations exception for this: it is never used to contact a child, build a profile or show ads. If we learn that an email came from a child under 13, we will delete it and not use the address for anything else.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising, as California law defines those terms.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the European Union, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of personal information about you. The site holds nothing we can link to you. If you have emailed us, write from the same address and we will send you a copy of what we hold or delete it, subject to the same limit as above.

Where EU or UK law applies, Penny Paws Games is the controller for the technical information Cloudflare handles for us and for the emails you send us. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a safe website and in answering you, and you may object to that use. This information is handled in the United States; Cloudflare and Google transfer data under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. You may complain to your data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Changes to this policy

When we change how the site handles information, we will update this page and its effective date. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
